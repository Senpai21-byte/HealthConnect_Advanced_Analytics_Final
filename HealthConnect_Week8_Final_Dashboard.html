What this means

For visits booked within a week, an SMS reminder barely changes the outcome (1.7 points). For visits booked 46–60 days ahead, the gap between SMS and no reminder grows to 10.7 points. Reminder effort should be aimed at long-lead bookings first.

Previous no-shows predict the next one

Each prior missed visit adds roughly 6–10 points of risk. Groups with fewer than 30 appointments are suppressed under the Week 7 minimum-sample rule.

Caveat for Data Science: only 13.8% of multi-visit patients have a previous_no_shows value that matches their actual history at each visit, so this field is a strong descriptive signal but is not yet verified as a safe real-time model input.

Where missed appointments are concentrated

Excess no-shows = appointments in the segment × how far its rate sits above the 51.2% baseline.

Known limitations

The data is synthetic, so the 51% baseline is not a real clinic benchmark; use the comparisons, not the absolute level. Lost slot rate is an upper bound because cancellations have no timestamp. Findings are associations, not proven causes; the reminder pilot is how causation should be tested.
